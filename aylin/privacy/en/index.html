Privacy Policy for Aylin

Effective Date: 1 October 2026 Developer / Controller: omniport (Serhii Kolodiazhnyi, individual developer, Ukraine) Contact: waw4303@gmail.com

1. Introduction & Core Privacy Principles

Welcome to Aylin (package: com.aylin.periodtracker), an intuitive menstrual cycle, fertility, and reproductive health tracker developed by omniport ("we", "us", or "our"). We believe that your intimate reproductive and personal health data is among the most sensitive information you possess, and it belongs solely to you.

Our app is built on three core technical and architectural commitments:

  1. No Accounts, No Tracking Servers: We do not operate our own database servers or user accounts. You never register an email address, username, phone number, or social media profile to use the app.
  2. Local-First & Hardware-Encrypted Storage: All your cycle logs, symptoms, moods, temperatures, intimacy records, and personal notes are stored locally on your device inside an encrypted SQL database protected by hardware-backed cryptographic keys.
  3. You Control Your Data: Cloud backups, Health Connect integration, and data exports are strictly opt-in and controlled entirely by you. You can wipe all data from your device at any moment with a single action.

This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use Aylin, how it is handled, and your rights under applicable privacy laws, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

2. Information We Do Not Collect on Developer Servers

Because Aylin operates without a developer server infrastructure:

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your health data to any server controlled by omniport.
  • We do not maintain user accounts, profiles, or identity databases.
  • We do not track your real-time physical GPS location.
  • We do not integrate third-party mobile analytics SDKs or crash-reporting services (such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, or Bugsnag).

3. Data Processed Locally on Your Device

All personal information entered into Aylin is stored exclusively in local device storage within an encrypted SQLite database (SQLCipher 256-bit AES encryption):

  • Menstrual & Cycle Information: Period start and end dates, cycle length, bleeding flow intensity (spotting, light, medium, heavy), and luteal phase adjustments.
  • Fertility & Reproductive Health: Basal body temperature (BBT), cervical mucus consistency, ovulation predictor kit (LH) test results, and pregnancy test results.
  • Physical Symptoms & Emotional Well-Being: Logs of physical symptoms (e.g., cramps, headaches, bloating, tender breasts), energy levels, and moods chosen from our symptom catalog.
  • Contraception & Medications: Pill tracking, patches, vaginal rings, injections, IUD reminders, and medication/supplement dosage notes.
  • Intimate Activity: Optional protected or unprotected intimacy records. This feature can be hidden completely by enabling Simple Mode.
  • Life Stage Logs:
  • Pregnancy Mode: Estimated due date, gestational age, kick counter logs, contraction timer logs, and maternal weight.
  • Perimenopause Mode: Hot flashes, night sweats, cycle irregularities, and mood shifts.
  • Application Preferences & Security Settings: Selected color theme, units of measurement (Celsius/Fahrenheit, kg/lbs), reminder schedules, app lock PIN hash, and camouflage icon selection.

4. On-Device Security & Architecture

We implement industry-standard cryptographic protections to safeguard your data on your device:

  • Hardware-Backed Database Encryption: Your local database is encrypted using SQLCipher with a 256-bit AES key. This encryption key is wrapped by the Android Keystore system using an AES-256-GCM master key that cannot be exported from the device's secure hardware.
  • Screen Protection (FLAG_SECURE): Aylin enables FLAG_SECURE on application screens, which prevents accidental screen captures, video recording by other applications, and hides your sensitive health screen thumbnails from the Android Recent Apps / Multitasking switcher.
  • App Lock (PIN & Biometrics): You can protect application access with a personal PIN (salted with PBKDF2 and Keystore pepper) or biometric authentication (Fingerprint / Face Unlock via Android BiometricPrompt).
  • Camouflage Launcher Icons & Discreet Notifications: You can disguise the app on your home screen by choosing an alternative icon (such as Calendar, Notes, or Weather) and enable discreet reminder notifications that omit sensitive reproductive terms.
  • Excluded from Android Auto Backup: Aylin explicitly sets android:allowBackup="false" and defines strict data extraction rules to prevent unencrypted device backups or automatic cloud transfers from copying your health database.

5. Third-Party Services & Integrations

Aylin interacts with third-party software development kits (SDKs) and platform services solely to provide essential operational features. Each service is described transparently below:

5.1 Google AdMob & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

  • Purpose: Non-paying users receive banner, interstitial, or user-initiated rewarded advertisements to support ongoing development.
  • Strictly Non-Personalized Ads Only: The application architecture strictly requests only non-personalized advertisements (npa=1 and PublisherPrivacyPersonalizationState.DISABLED). The app never requests personalized, targeted, or behavioral advertisements under any circumstances.
  • Data Processed: The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / GAID), coarse location derived from IP address, app interaction metrics, and basic technical diagnostic telemetry in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Play Data Disclosure.
  • Privacy & Consent (UMP): In regions where consent is legally required (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog is displayed to obtain required regulatory consent prior to making any ad requests.
  • Strict Health Data Barrier: No reproductive health data, cycle logs, intimate activity, symptoms, or personal health notes are ever transmitted to or shared with Google AdMob or advertising partners.
  • Ad Removal: Users who purchase the "Remove Ads" one-time purchase or maintain an active Aylin Premium subscription have all advertising SDK calls completely and permanently disabled.

5.2 Google Play In-App Billing

  • Purpose: Manages subscription purchases (monthly, yearly), lifetime access, and ad-removal purchases.
  • Data Processed: Payment transactions are handled directly and securely by Google Play (Google LLC). omniport receives only anonymous order tokens and purchase state confirmations to unlock Premium features. We never see, receive, or store your credit card number, bank details, or billing address.

5.3 Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)

  • Purpose: Provides user-initiated encrypted backup and restore across device transitions.
  • How It Works: Cloud backup is completely optional and disabled by default. If you choose to enable it, Aylin connects to your personal Google Drive account via Google Sign-In and stores backup files strictly within the hidden application data folder (appDataFolder).
  • Encryption: Backup archives are encrypted using AES-GCM before upload. Automatic backups are encrypted with a device-derived key; manual backups are protected with a user-chosen password. omniport has no access to your Google account, Drive files, or encryption passwords.

5.4 Google Health Connect (Optional)

  • Purpose: Allows two-way synchronization of health, menstrual, and vital measurements with other health apps on your device that support Google Health Connect.
  • Granular Control: Integration is completely optional and disabled by default. You grant or deny read and write permissions individually for each supported data type via the Android system permission interface.
  • Supported Data Types:
  • Menstruation Flow & Periods: Read and write bleeding intensity and period duration.
  • Basal Body Temperature: Read and write morning temperature logs.
  • Body Weight: Read and write weight records.
  • Sleep Duration: Read-only import of sleep session duration into daily metrics (Aylin does not write sleep sessions).
  • Cervical Mucus & Ovulation Tests: Read and write fertility indicators (opt-in).
  • Sexual Activity: Read and write intimacy timing (opt-in, disabled by default, and suppressed in Simple Mode).

6. Health Connect Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

Aylin's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Google Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  1. User-Facing Functionality Only: We use data accessed through Health Connect exclusively to provide and improve user-facing features within Aylin, specifically calculating cycle lengths, predicting upcoming periods and ovulation, and visualizing wellness trends.
  2. No Third-Party Transfers: We do not transfer Health Connect data to third parties, except where strictly required to provide user-facing features with your consent, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a corporate reorganization or merger where user consent is obtained.
  3. No Advertising: Health Connect data is never used or transferred to third parties for advertising purposes, including personalized advertising, interest-based targeting, or retargeting.
  4. No Sale of Health Data: We never sell, license, or monetize Health Connect data under any circumstances.
  5. No AI/ML Training: Health Connect data is never used to train, retrain, or fine-tune general artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning models.
  6. No Human Access: Human access to Health Connect data is strictly prohibited unless:
  • We have obtained your explicit consent to inspect specific data for technical troubleshooting;
  • It is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating security vulnerabilities); or
  • It is required to comply with mandatory applicable law.

8. Data Retention, Export, and Complete Deletion

You retain full ownership and control over your data retention schedule:

  • Local Retention: Your health logs remain stored on your physical device until you delete individual entries, perform a complete data wipe, or uninstall the application.
  • Export Formats: You may export your logs at any time as an encrypted Aylin archive, a standard CSV file, an iCalendar (.ics) file, or generate a PDF summary for your healthcare provider.
  • Complete In-App Data Wipe ("Delete All Data"): Navigating to Settings and selecting "Delete all data" immediately and irreversibly destroys the local SQLCipher database, purges Keystore encryption keys, clears local preferences, and resets the application.
  • Health Connect Revocation & Purge: You can disconnect Health Connect at any time via Settings or Android system settings. Selecting "Disconnect & delete synced data" purges records previously written by Aylin from the Health Connect repository.

9. Your Legal Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your geographical jurisdiction, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your personal information:

9.1 European Union & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Right of Access & Portability: You can access all your logged records directly within the app screens or export them into open CSV, ICS, or PDF formats.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit or correct any past cycle date, symptom, or note directly in the Day Log calendar view.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can delete individual log entries or execute the in-app "Delete all data" command to permanently destroy all records.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing relies on consent (e.g., UMP ad personalization, Health Connect sync, Google Drive backup), you can withdraw consent at any time via application settings or Android system settings.
  • Contacting a Supervisory Authority: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority.

9.2 California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

  • Right to Know and Access: You have the right to know what personal information is collected and to access it through the app.
  • Right to Delete: You can delete your personal information directly using the in-app deletion tools.
  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Data: We do not sell your personal information or sensitive health data, nor do we share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your statutory CCPA/CPRA rights.

Because omniport does not maintain remote user accounts or retain user data on developer servers, you can exercise the majority of your rights autonomously and immediately inside the application without submitting an identity verification request. For any questions, contact waw4303@gmail.com.
